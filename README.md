Measure Twice is a room and floor-plan tool for real renovations. Draw a room to scale, or type in its exact dimensions, then switch between a 2D plan and a 3D view to see how it will actually look.

Add furniture from a built-in library, including common IKEA products, or build your own from scratch. Check clearances, door swings, and wall space before you buy or build anything. Group rooms into a full floor plan and arrange them to match your house.

Measure Twice runs entirely in your browser. Your room plans are saved to your own device — nobody else can see them, and there's no account to set up.
